# 📈 Business Recommendations

## Objective

Provide actionable recommendations to help Cyclistic convert more casual riders into annual members.

---

## 1. Target Weekend Riders

**Observation**
- Casual riders are more active on weekends and take longer rides.

**Recommendation**
- Launch weekend promotions through social media, email, and the mobile app.

**Expected Impact**
- Increase membership conversions among leisure riders.

---

## 2. Focus on Peak Seasons

**Observation**
- Ride activity is highest from May to September.

**Recommendation**
- Promote memberships before and during the summer season with limited-time offers.

**Expected Impact**
- Reach more active users and improve conversion rates.

---

## 3. Personalize Digital Marketing

**Observation**
- Casual riders are most active during the afternoon.

**Recommendation**
- Send targeted promotions during peak riding hours using digital channels.

**Expected Impact**
- Improve engagement and encourage membership purchases.

---

# Conclusion

The analysis shows that casual riders are mainly leisure users with longer rides and higher weekend activity. Targeted seasonal and digital marketing campaigns could help convert them into annual members.
